What This Calculator Does

This calculator estimates your Ontario income tax for the 2026 tax year. Enter your annual gross employment income, and it computes each deduction step by step. Federal income tax is calculated using CRA progressive brackets ranging from 14% to 33%, with the basic personal amount applied as a non-refundable credit. Ontario provincial income tax applies brackets from 5.05% to 13.16%, and the Ontario surtax is added when your basic provincial tax exceeds the first surtax threshold. Canada Pension Plan contributions are calculated under both CPP1 (5.95% on pensionable earnings between $3,500 and $74,600) and CPP2 (4.00% on earnings between $74,600 and $85,000). Employment Insurance premiums are set at 1.63% on insurable earnings up to the annual maximum of $68,900. The Ontario Health Premium is applied based on taxable income tiers. Ontario's basic personal amount is also applied as a non-refundable credit. Results display your federal tax, Ontario tax with surtax, total deductions, net income, effective tax rate, and marginal rates so you can understand exactly how your income is taxed.

What Is Not Included

Results are estimates for a typical Ontario employee with a single employer and may not reflect your actual tax obligation. This calculator does not account for RRSP contributions that reduce taxable income, childcare expense deductions, union dues, registered pension plan contributions, or other workplace deductions. It also does not include charitable donation credits, tuition or medical expense credits, spousal or dependent amounts, the Canada Workers Benefit, disability credits, or other individual tax credits that may lower your tax bill. Investment income such as eligible or non-eligible dividends, capital gains, and rental income are not handled. Self-employment income and non-standard pay arrangements are also outside scope. If you have multiple income sources, variable compensation, or situation-specific deductions, your actual tax will differ from the estimate shown. For detailed questions about tax brackets, the Ontario surtax, the Health Premium, how raises and overtime affect your tax, and other payroll topics, see the FAQ. For precise calculations, consult a tax professional or use the CRA Payroll Deductions Online Calculator.

Example: $110,000 Annual Salary

For an Ontario employee earning $110,000 gross, using 2026 rates, this income enters the third federal bracket and triggers the first Ontario surtax tier. The Ontario Health Premium applies at the $300 level. After federal tax, Ontario tax with surtax, CPP1, CPP2, EI, and OHP, the estimated net income is approximately $77,570 with an effective combined rate around 29.5%. At this income level, the marginal federal rate is 20.5% and the marginal Ontario rate including surtax is noticeably higher than the base 9.15% bracket. Your actual result will vary based on pay structure and applicable credits. Enter your income in the calculator above for a personalized breakdown of every bracket and deduction.
